# ⚠️ EstateIQ — Known Limitations, Assumptions & Governance Disclaimers

**Reference: `EstateIQ_Final_Round_Implementation_Report.pdf` (Version 1.0, 29 October 2026)**

This document establishes the official operational boundaries, assumptions, and ethical guardrails of the EstateIQ Sustainable Facility & Estate Intelligence platform.

---

## 1. Prototype Status & Demonstration Data

1. **Synthetic & Simulated Telemetry**:
   - Telemetry signals (energy consumption, water flow, air quality PM2.5, waste bin levels) generated by the built-in simulator or seed scripts are **SYNTHETIC** or **SIMULATED**.
   - These records model statistical patterns and physical behaviors of an Indian engineering campus for repeatable evaluation. They are **not official utility bills, certified environmental measurements, or government filings**.
   - All charts, tables, API responses, and reports explicitly display provenance badges (`[SYNTHETIC IoT]`, `[CSV IMPORT]`, `[SIMULATED]`).

2. **No Claim of 100% Real-World Accuracy**:
   - A passing test suite or demo run proves that the software prototype functions end-to-end as specified. It does **not** prove 100% forecasting accuracy or zero false-positive anomaly detection on uncalibrated physical sensors.

---

## 2. Decision Support vs. Physical Automation (Human-in-the-Loop)

1. **No Automatic Equipment Actuation**:
   - EstateIQ is strictly an **advisory decision-support system**.
   - It **does not** automatically actuate or manipulate physical HVAC equipment, water pumps, electrical breakers, valves, or municipal waste dispatchers.
   - All recommendations require explicit human review, authorization, and operational assignment by a qualified facility engineer or administrator.

2. **Causation vs. Correlation (SHAP & Feature Attribution)**:
   - Feature attribution methods (such as SHAP TreeExplainer) quantify how input variables influenced the statistical model's output for a given observation.
   - **SHAP values do not prove physical or mechanical causation.** An elevated SHAP score indicates an association within the trained model, not guaranteed physical root cause. Explanations use qualifying language: *"associated with / contributed to model output"* and require physical on-site verification.

---

## 3. Financial Scenarios & Sustainability Scoring

1. **What-If Scenarios are Estimates, Not Verified Savings**:
   - Energy what-if scenario simulations calculate proportional mathematical estimates:
     $$\text{Estimated Energy Delta (kWh)} = \text{Forecast Baseline (kWh)} \times \text{Assumed Reduction Fraction}$$
     $$\text{Estimated Cost Delta (₹)} = \text{Estimated Energy Delta} \times \text{Configurable Tariff (₹9.50/kWh)}$$
   - These figures are scenario calculations based on user-supplied assumptions. They are labeled **"Estimate, not verified savings"** and must not be presented as contractual or realized cost reductions until post-intervention baseline measurement and verification (M&V) is complete.

2. **Prototype Sustainability Scorecard**:
   - The campus sustainability scorecard (0–100) is an internal operational benchmark computed from normalized intensity metrics, waste fill rates, and air threshold compliance.
   - It is **not** an official LEED, GRIHA, BEE Star Rating, or government regulatory compliance certification.

---

## 4. Hardware, Network & Offline Resilience

1. **Local-First & Offline Operation**:
   - The core local demo operates 100% offline without internet access, external cloud servers, or third-party GenAI LLM APIs.
   - When external LLM services or API keys are unavailable, the platform seamlessly falls back to deterministic rule-based recommendation templates.

2. **Physical IoT Gateways**:
   - In production deployment, physical smart meters and sub-metering hardware (Modbus RTU, BACnet/IP, MQTT broker) communicate through an authenticated local gateway. Telemetry ingested from unauthenticated sources is rejected by server-side validation.
